Sum up 22 November's world news in one image
([news-image-20261122](https://make.fun/bounty/news-image-20261122)): a
front-page style PNG of Sunday 22 November 2026 — NATO rallies behind Poland
after Russian missiles hit its soil, Mexican forces capture cartel kingpin
"El Chapo Isidro" as 11 are killed in the backlash, the EU and Indonesia sign
a mega trade deal after a decade of talks, drones hit a Russian cargo ship in
the Baltic, and Iran strikes spread across the Gulf, plus COP31, the
Japan–India training pact, trilateral peace talks in Abu Dhabi, and more. The
image is rendered by `generate.py` (Pillow, no network, deterministic) and
copied to `/output/entry.png` by `main.ts`.

## Run it

```sh
mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out node main.ts
```

To re-render the image from scratch (needs Pillow):

```sh
python3 generate.py
```
